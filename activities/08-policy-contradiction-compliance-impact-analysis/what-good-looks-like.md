**Expected output structure**
A strong AI output for this activity will contain the following sections / elements:
- 3+ specific contradictions with paragraph-level citations from both documents
- Compliance mapping: each contradiction tied to specific RBI §
- Severity ranking: which contradiction is driving the 15% error spike (temporal evidence)
- Draft unified SOP structure with forcing-function guardrails (not reminders)
- Executive brief: top contradiction + compliance risk + remediation plan
**Scoring rubric (5 dimensions)**
**Common failure modes — watch for these**
- Model hallucinates contradictions. Response: 'read §X.Y aloud from the SOP. Does it actually say what you claimed?'
- Compliance mapping is generic. Response: 'cite the specific RBI § clause being violated'.
- Remediation is reminder-based. Response: 'reminders do not work. What is the forcing function?'
**Debrief question for the room**
Where did the AI add the most value — and where would you NOT trust it without a human review step? Be specific about the failure mode you saw.
Scoring is a facilitation device — not an exam. The point is to make the quality dimensions visible and discussable, so participants internalize what a 'good' AI output looks like for their own work.
