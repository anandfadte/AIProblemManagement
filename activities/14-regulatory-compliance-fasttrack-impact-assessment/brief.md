**The ask**
**Produce the DPDP Act compliance impact matrix: which current operations are compliant, which are not, cost of compliance, timeline. Red Team identifies missed obligations.**
**Context**
The DPDP Act 2023 is in phased implementation. Phase 1 effective 1-Jan-2026 (consent, grievance, breach notification). Phase 2 effective 1-Jul-2026 (data principal rights, retention, DPO). Phase 3 effective 1-Jan-2027 (cross-border, DPIA, independent audit).

The current data handling SOP + consent policy + data processing inventory have known gaps. Three AI analysts independently read the regulation + current state + produce an impact matrix. Red Team identifies obligations the three analysts missed — specifically the children's data obligations which are easy to miss but carry ₹200cr penalty.
**Why this matters — the wow moment**
Three AIs produce three different impact matrices. All three catch the consent-architecture change, retention gap, and cross-border transfer risk. Red Team: 'none of three flagged children's data (DP007 in inventory). DPDP Act requires verifiable parental consent — your current process does not have digital verification. Penalty up to ₹200 crore.'
