## Why This Activity

**This proves the pattern works beyond IT.** The same multi-tool pipeline that mines ITSM incidents also cracks a finance reconciliation problem. Different domain, same discipline — and it introduces multimodal analysis where Gemini reads a chart image without seeing the underlying data.

### What You Will Learn

- **Sequential 4-tool pipeline** — ChatGPT does variance math (quantitative only, no causes). Gemini reads the trend chart PNG (multimodal — image, no spreadsheet). Claude produces the causal RCA using the control framework. Mistral writes the Controller-grade close brief.
- **Tool discipline under pressure** — ChatGPT must NOT explain causes. Gemini must NOT see the data. Claude must NOT invent controls. Each tool has a hard boundary.
- **Chain coherence check** — if ChatGPT finds GL-3002 as the top variance but Claude's RCA explains GL-4001, the chain has *drifted*. You restart Claude's step.

### Why It Matters for Problem Management

Problem Management is not just an IT discipline. Finance reconciliation failures, compliance gaps, and operational variances all follow the same causal-analysis pattern. This activity demonstrates that the AI orchestration skills transfer directly to cross-functional problem domains.

### Facilitator Notes

- **The multimodal step (Gemini reading the chart) is deliberately constrained.** Gemini only gets the image — not the spreadsheet. This teaches independent verification: does what the chart shows match what the numbers say?
- If participants give Gemini both the image AND the data, the teaching moment is lost. Enforce the constraint.
- The chain drift check (Step 5) is where most groups catch their first mistake. Celebrate it — catching drift is a skill, not a failure.
- The deliverable is a **1-page Controller-grade brief**. If it is longer than 1 page, it failed. The CFO gets 300 words, not 3,000.
- **Time check:** The Mistral brief should take under 5 minutes. If it is taking longer, the group likely gave Mistral too much raw output instead of Claude's curated RCA.
