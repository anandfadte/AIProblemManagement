## Why This Activity

**This is your foundation.** Every multi-AI pattern you will use in this studio starts here. You learn the core discipline: give each AI tool a specialist role, feed it structured data, and chain outputs into a pipeline where each step builds on the last.

### What You Will Learn

- **Multi-tool Pipeline pattern** — the workhorse of AI orchestration. ChatGPT clusters 2,500 ITSM tickets by pattern. Gemini reads a Grafana alert screenshot (multimodal — image, not data). Claude cross-references findings with the change log. Mistral writes the CIO brief.
- **Role assignment** — why giving an AI a specific role ("Senior ITSM Data Analyst") produces sharper output than a generic ask.
- **Chain discipline** — if the output of Step 2 does not feed cleanly into Step 3, you restart Step 2. No patching, no workarounds.

### Why It Matters for Problem Management

Pattern mining is the first thing a Problem Manager does after a major incident wave: *"Are these 47 tickets the same problem, or four different ones?"* Getting this wrong means chasing four RCAs when one would do — or worse, writing one RCA when there are four distinct root causes.

### Facilitator Notes

- This is the **warm-up activity** — use it to calibrate the group's comfort with AI tools before moving to more complex patterns.
- The multimodal step (Gemini reading the Grafana screenshot) is the "wow moment" — let participants see AI read an image they would normally eyeball.
- Common mistake: participants try to give ChatGPT the Grafana image too. Reinforce: right tool for the right job.
- **Time check:** If a group finishes in under 20 minutes, they likely skipped the cross-referencing step. Ask them to verify.
