**The ask**
**Produce a 5-slide executive deck for Monday's CEO Weekly Operating Review. Three AI models draft different variants; the fourth synthesizes the winning narrative.**
**Context**
The CEO's Weekly Operating Review (WOR) is Monday 09:00. Max 10 agenda items. Each slot: 2 minutes to brief, 10 minutes discussion. The CEO reads decks longer than 5 slides on paper, not live.

This activity uses role-differentiated multi-AI drafting. Each AI brings a different frame:
- ChatGPT drafts the TACTICAL variant (operational focus: what happened, what we did, what's next)
- Claude drafts the STRATEGIC variant (long-term implications)
- Gemini drafts the RISK-FOCUSED variant (what could go wrong + mitigation)
Then Mistral synthesizes the winning narrative that integrates the strongest 2 of 5 slides from each.
**Why this matters — the wow moment**
Three AI models each draft a 5-slide deck from the SAME findings, but with different framing. The three decks are materially different — different leads, different call-outs, different closing asks. Mistral integrates. The CEO gets ONE deck that has tactical depth + strategic framing + risk mitigation.
