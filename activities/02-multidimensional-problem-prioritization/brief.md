**The ask**
**Rank 18 candidate operational problems for Q3-Q4 investment prioritization. Make the customer-impact vs financial-impact weighting trade-off explicit. The executive owns the weighting decision.**
**Context**
The quarterly Operations Steering Committee allocates investment across 15-20 candidate operational problems. The weighting between customer-impact and financial-impact dimensions is contested — the CEO memo (business_impact_context.docx) states customer impact is the Q3-Q4 priority, but the CFO's standing practice is equal weighting.

The exercise uses TWO AI tools adversarially: Claude produces a transparent scoring + sensitivity analysis, ChatGPT then attacks Claude's weighting choices, and the executive owns the final decision. The point is NOT that one AI is right. The point is that the AI exposes the trade-off, and the human owns the weighting call.
**Why this matters — the wow moment**
Claude proposes a scoring. ChatGPT then challenges Claude's weighting ('you weighted recurrence equally with customer-impact — the CEO memo says customer-impact is Q3 priority — justify or re-weight'). The room watches two AIs disagree in a structured way. The executive picks the final weighting live.
