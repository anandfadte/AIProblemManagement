**Expected output structure**
A strong AI output for this activity will contain the following sections / elements:
- ChatGPT PERFORMANCE: 24-month trend + SLA breach count + credit calculation + 'one-off vs systemic' call
- Claude DEPENDENCY & ALTERNATIVES: our-side dependency map + ranked alternatives + switch cost + timeline
- Gemini CONTRACT: remedies available (SLA credit, termination for cause, transition assistance) + legal basis for each
- Mistral RED TEAM: concentration risk — vendor handles 65% of APAC CNP with no parallel arrangement
- Final executive decision matrix: Continue / Renegotiate / Parallelize / Terminate with reasoning
**Scoring rubric (5 dimensions)**
**Common failure modes — watch for these**
- Performance analyst accepts 'one-off' narrative. Response: 'the 24-month trend shows DEGRADATION. Does the data support one-off?'
- Alternative analysis is a list, not a ranking. Response: 'rank 1-5 by strategic fit AND switch cost AND timeline. Which is actually the best alternative?'
- Red Team focuses on the outage, not the concentration. Response: 'read internal_impact_assessment Downstream Vendor Dependency Map section. What is the systemic risk the outage exposed?'
**Debrief question for the room**
Where did the AI add the most value — and where would you NOT trust it without a human review step? Be specific about the failure mode you saw.
Scoring is a facilitation device — not an exam. The point is to make the quality dimensions visible and discussable, so participants internalize what a 'good' AI output looks like for their own work.
