**Expected output structure**
A strong AI output for this activity will contain the following sections / elements:
- Three independent scoring matrices (ChatGPT, Claude-with-skill, Gemini)
- Consensus on top-2 productization candidates (typically PAT-02 and PAT-05)
- Red Team commercial challenge — specifically vendor-dependency risk
- Final executive investment decision: which 2 patterns, with INR investment thesis
**Scoring rubric (5 dimensions)**
**Common failure modes — watch for these**
- Scorings are uniform across all 5 patterns. Response: 'differentiate — rank from strongest to weakest, don't score everything 7-8.'
- Red Team misses vendor risk. Response: 'PAT-02 depends on 4 AI tools remaining accessible. What if OpenAI restricts API? What if Anthropic changes terms?'
- No specific INR investment ask. Response: 'the Chief Commercial Officer needs to see: INR X investment, Y team, Z months to revenue, projected ARR'.
**Debrief question for the room**
Where did the AI add the most value — and where would you NOT trust it without a human review step? Be specific about the failure mode you saw.
Scoring is a facilitation device — not an exam. The point is to make the quality dimensions visible and discussable, so participants internalize what a 'good' AI output looks like for their own work.
