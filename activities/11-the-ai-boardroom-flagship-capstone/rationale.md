## Why This Activity

**This is the capstone — everything comes together.** Five AI tools, five specialist roles, one fresh P2 incident the group has never seen. No scaffolding, no practice data. This is as close to real-world AI-augmented Problem Management as you get in a training room.

### What You Will Learn

- **Full 5-tool orchestration** — ChatGPT as Data Analyst, Claude as RCA Investigator, Gemini as Evidence Integrator, Mistral as Contrarian, and a second Claude session as the Synthesizer who writes the final board brief.
- **Pipeline + Council hybrid** — Steps 2-4 run as a pipeline (data → hypothesis → evidence). Step 5 is adversarial (Mistral challenges the emerging consensus). Step 6 synthesizes into a single executive narrative.
- **Executive communication** — the deliverable is not a technical RCA; it is a board-ready brief that a CIO can present in 3 minutes.

### Why It Matters for Problem Management

In a real war room, the Problem Manager does not have one AI — they have a team of specialists. This activity trains the judgment of *when to hand off*, *what each tool does best*, and *how to maintain chain coherence* when five tools are producing output simultaneously.

### Facilitator Notes

- **This is 40 minutes.** Groups that try to rush will produce a disjointed brief. Enforce the pipeline: no skipping to the synthesis before the evidence integration is complete.
- The "wow moment" is Step 5 (Mistral Contrarian). If Mistral finds a flaw the first four tools missed, the group must decide: restart the chain, or acknowledge the gap in the brief?
- The Synthesizer (Step 6) must cite evidence from *all four prior tools*. A synthesis that only references Claude's RCA is not a synthesis — it is a rewrite.
- **Common failure:** Groups give the Synthesizer all raw outputs instead of the curated evidence package. The result is bloated and unfocused. Coach them: curate before you synthesize.
- This activity works best when participants have already completed Activity 01 (pipeline) and Activity 03 (council). If they have not, expect 50+ minutes.
