**Expected output structure**
A strong AI output for this activity will contain the following sections / elements:
- ChatGPT DATA ANALYST output: when + what + observations
- Claude RCA INVESTIGATOR output: 5-hypothesis register
- Gemini EVIDENCE INTEGRATOR output: timeline + evidence inventory
- Mistral CONTRARIAN output: what three analysts are missing
- Claude SYNTHESIZER output: consensus scorecard + Red Team + executive recommendation
- Live Drive folder — all 5 outputs visible to the room
- Executive decision captured in /06_Executive_Deliverables/
**Scoring rubric (5 dimensions)**
**Common failure modes — watch for these**
- An AI breaks role (DATA ANALYST starts proposing hypotheses). Response: 'stay in your role — hypotheses are the RCA investigator's job'.
- Contrarian is generic. Response: 'specifically — what in the evidence pack did the three analysts not reference?'
- Synthesizer loses the Red Team's challenge. Response: 'the Red Team found X. Your synthesis must address X, not restate the consensus.'
**Debrief question for the room**
Where did the AI add the most value — and where would you NOT trust it without a human review step? Be specific about the failure mode you saw.
Scoring is a facilitation device — not an exam. The point is to make the quality dimensions visible and discussable, so participants internalize what a 'good' AI output looks like for their own work.
